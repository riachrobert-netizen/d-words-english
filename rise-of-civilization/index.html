Build a Civilization

Start as hunter-gatherers, enter the Neolithic Age, then build a Bronze Age civilization.

  • Select a villager, then click trees, deer, berries, stone, or gold.
  • Workers carry resources back to the Town Center or nearest Storehouse.
  • Action buttons turn green when available.
  • For buildings, choose the building button, move the preview over the map, then click a valid location.
  • Green footprint = valid. Red footprint = blocked. Press Esc to cancel.
  • In the Neolithic Age, build a Barracks for spearmen. In the Bronze Age, train swordsmen and build an Archery Range for archers. Build a bridge across the river to reach your opponent.

Win: Conquest means destroying the enemy Town Center and all its people. If enabled, Civilization Victory requires all six feature boxes below the map. Houses and farms support settlement growth.
